# Code of Conduct

Orakzai Bond aims to maintain a respectful, constructive, and safety-conscious environment for contributors and community participants.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and considerate of differing experience and viewpoints.
- Give actionable feedback focused on ideas and work, not personal attributes.
- Communicate accurately; distinguish verified facts from assumptions and proposals.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish credentials, private communications, personal information, or vulnerability details.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, doxxing, targeted insults, sexualized conduct without consent, deliberate disruption, and knowingly misleading claims are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact route on the [official website](https://orakzaibond.com), including the relevant repository or discussion and a concise description. Do not use public issues to disclose sensitive personal information. Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation where reasonably necessary. Decisions will be based on the available information and applied proportionately.

This code applies to project spaces and when someone is representing the project in community spaces.
